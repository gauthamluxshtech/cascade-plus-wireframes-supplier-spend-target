Settings

Options

General Limit

Applies to every product that does not have a product-specific limit.

Unsaved changes

The general limit is off. Only products with a product-specific limit show a warning in the Order Pad.

When an item's quantity for today (orders placed plus the basket) goes above its limit, its Order Pad line shows a warning sign. Hover over the sign for the details.

The warning never blocks an order. Adding, editing and placing orders work as before.

Product Specific Limit

Set a different quantity limit for selected products. It replaces the general limit for those products, and still applies when the general limit is off.

Product PIP Code Quantity Limit Last Changed Actions

Drug Tariff Price Override

Allow supplier prices up to a set percentage above Drug Tariff.

Override limit above Drug Tariff

Enter a percentage from 1 to 50, in steps of 0.5 (for example 7.5).

Unsaved changes

Suppliers priced within your override limit become available to cascade, by default it still tries the cheapest supplier first.

Prices allowed by the override are marked DT so you can see them before ordering.

Supplier Lock

Lock a supplier so the Order Pad never cascades to it. Switch a lock on, then optionally schedule when it applies. Changes apply once saved.

Supplier Status Lock Schedule
Unsaved changes

A lock with no schedule starts when it is saved and stays on until it is switched off. Use Schedule to lock between two dates, or on chosen days every week.

Locked suppliers are skipped by the cascade. In Edit Product they are shown as Locked and cannot be selected.

Supplier Spend Targets

Set the most you want to spend with each supplier.

Supplier Spend Target Maximum Cap Schedule
Unsaved changes

Choose how often the cap starts again: weekly, monthly or quarterly, or on specific dates.

A spend target never blocks an order or changes the cascade. Progress shows in the Supplier Target Report and on the Dashboard.
