    How projected spend is calculated

    Projected spend estimates what each supplier's spend will be at the end of its current period, if ordering carries on at the same daily rate as so far.

    Projected = Spend So Far ÷ Days Gone × Days in Period
    • Spend Against Caps is the total spent with the listed suppliers in their current periods.
